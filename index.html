Finance manager summary — April 2026
The office closed April 2026 with a net surplus of $1.04M, driven by strong Global Centre and Direct Funding receipts. However, three areas require management attention: cash position remains 65% below the target low at $181K (vs. $521K target); admin cost rate has crept to 10.6% against a 9.49% target, primarily from technical support and CLU cost overruns; and ADP Bukidnon is the only project in overspend at 109% YTD driven by shared NO/FO costs rather than direct programme costs. The majority of underspending across other projects is attributable to rescheduled activities, integration savings, and deferred implementation — all with catch-up plans in place for Q2.
