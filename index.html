Finance manager summary — Period ending April 2026
The office closed April 2026 with a net surplus of $1.04M, driven by strong Global Centre and Direct Funding receipts. However, three areas require management attention: cash position remains 65% below the target low at $181K (vs. $521K target); admin cost rate stands at 10.6% against a 9.49% target, with cost reduction in support costs ongoing; and ADP Bukidnon, ADP Albay, Camarines Sur ADP, and Antique 3 are in overspend, primarily driven by advance implementation of activities, higher SNC kit costs, and staff/technical cost absorption. The majority of underspending across other projects is attributable to rescheduled activities, deferred learning kit deliveries ahead of school year opening, IPF integration transition timelines, and procurement adjustments — all with catch-up plans in place.
